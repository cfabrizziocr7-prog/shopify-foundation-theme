# Sloane voice
Specific, calm, concise. Talk about an outfit and the actual gray cropped jacket.
Use: gray texture, silver zip, cropped cut, denim, trousers, coffee plans.
Avoid: timeless sophistication, must-have, viral bestseller, perfect fit, real wool, fake personal testimonials.
Audience hypothesis: shoppers looking for a considered fall layer. Validate with behavior.
No invented reviews, discounts, urgency or delivery guarantees. The existing compare price is not proof of historical sales.
One CTA per post. Brand-led demonstrations may use AI models but must not impersonate customers.
